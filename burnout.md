# Burnout

Burnout isn’t caused by crunch periods or a lack of PTO used.  
**Burnout is caused by unclear priorities and expectations.**

When you don’t know what’s coming next.  
When there’s no plan or vision. 
When the system is so fragile that there's an overwhelming bug backlog and constant pager alerts.

## Burnout Vanishes

When software is **designed** and **planned** with intention.   
When there’s a **shared vision** to align to.  
When priorities include quality (testing, monitoring, and observability).
