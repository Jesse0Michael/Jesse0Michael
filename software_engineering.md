# Software Engineering
> Software Engineering is what happens to programming when you add time and other people. - Russ Cox




### Not Invented Here
Writing custom software is the most expensive way for engineering to provide value.  
Before you invest in building and maintaining custom solutions, consider if the software is a core competency of your business. If its not, see if there are open source or commercial solutions that meets your needs.
